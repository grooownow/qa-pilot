## Not covered

The rule catalog in `knowledge/catalog/` is derived from a third-party book. No licence from
the licensor covers the book's own content, and commercial licences do not include that
catalog. The Agensi packs do not contain it. With a commercial licence, answer "commercial"
when onboarding asks (or put `use: commercial` under `## Licence` in
`docs/qa/project-profile.md`): the audits then run without the catalog.
